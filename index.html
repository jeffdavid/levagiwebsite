Intelligence with a center

Lev לֵב

Lev literally means “heart.” In biblical and traditional Hebrew thought, it is more than emotion: the lev is the core control center of a person, encompassing the mind, will, intellect, and moral decision-making.

AGI

Artificial general intelligence: AI that can understand, learn, and reason across a wide range of tasks, rather than doing just one narrow job.

Brought together, they make Levagi: capable AI guided by clear thinking, purposeful action, and sound judgment, the qualities the lev stands for.
